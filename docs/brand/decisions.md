## Editing protocol
When a decision changes, **append a new dated entry** describing what was superseded and why. Do not silently rewrite an uncertain direction as fact. Use “confirmed”, “approved direction”, “exploratory”, “historical”, or “undecided”; cite the founder's own words where reliable. Keep private information and details of family, finance, travel, and home security out of the public repository.
