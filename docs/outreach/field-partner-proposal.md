# TELO × Just Haus

## Cold-climate construction field-use proposal

### Purpose

Explore Just Haus as an early commercial MT1 customer and a possible field partner for sustained construction, winter, rural-charging, and property-energy-system use.

### About Just Haus

Just Haus is an early-stage design-build venture in Franconia, New Hampshire, focused on compact, highly efficient homes, durable materials, solar power, battery storage, resilience, and off-grid systems. The founder is an engineer, builder, experienced EV-truck owner, and off-grid energy-system user.

### Why this environment is useful

- Real residential construction: tools, lumber, sheet goods, materials, site visits, and towing within verified ratings.
- Northern conditions: deep cold, prolonged cold soak, snow, repeated icing, and winter charging.
- Difficult roads: White Mountain grades, rural access, and rough roads in Quebec and New Brunswick.
- Integrated energy context: solar generation, stationary batteries, inverters, finite service capacity, and off-grid load management.
- Long-term use: repeated routes and tasks across seasons, not a short demonstration.

### Why the operator is useful

Daniel brings engineering and building experience, established EV-truck ownership, and hands-on operation of off-grid energy systems. Years of winter Tesla use have exposed recurring cold-weather usability and reliability frustrations that can inform a more demanding test plan: not simply whether the vehicle functions, but whether charging, controls, access, traction, thermal behavior, and daily workflows remain dependable through a full northern winter.

Daniel is willing to contribute field-testing time and structured product feedback without consulting fees. This does not presume free use of a vehicle or personally funded program expenses; vehicle costs, direct expenses, insurance, liability, support, data rights, confidentiality, and formal deliverables would be agreed before any test program.

### Proposed field program

Establish an agreed measurement and feedback cadence around:

- Energy consumption by temperature, route, elevation, speed, and load.
- Loaded and unloaded efficiency on repeatable routes.
- Battery behavior, available power, preconditioning, regeneration, and charging after prolonged cold soak.
- Cargo, payload, towing, site access, traction, and ground-clearance observations.
- Windshield clearing, wiper performance, washer-fluid delivery, frozen handles, seals, latches, sensors, and tailgate or liftgate operation after snow and ice accumulation.
- Tool or temporary-load use only where officially supported by TELO.
- Solar-aware charging and home-energy load-management scenarios.

Any future vehicle-to-home or bidirectional testing would be considered only if TELO officially supports it and defines the equipment, safety requirements, and test scope.

### Deliverables to TELO

- Structured monthly field notes and issue reports.
- Agreed data exports or summary tables, with privacy boundaries defined in advance.
- Seasonal winter-performance summaries.
- Construction-use photography and concise case-study material.
- Direct product feedback with reproduction conditions and severity.
- Candid findings, including limitations and failure modes.

### Proposed first step

A 20-minute conversation to compare this use case with TELO’s commercial-launch, product-validation, customer-advisory, or case-study priorities. This proposal does not request preferential placement; it asks whether a genuine commercial use case can also create useful field learning.
