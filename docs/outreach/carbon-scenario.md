# Construction travel carbon and cost scenario

This note documents a supporting outreach scenario. The published concept page mentions the potential benefit only qualitatively. This is a screening calculation, not a formal emissions inventory or tax calculation.

## User-supplied operating pattern

- Two to three gasoline pickups in the conventional-build comparison.
- One 30–60 mile round trip per truck per workday.
- Five workdays per week.
- Six to twelve months, modeled as 26–52 weeks.
- Solo-led alternative uses one TELO charged from incremental onsite solar.

## Mileage

Per-truck build mileage:

`30–60 miles/day × 5 days/week × 26–52 weeks = 3,900–15,600 miles`

Total gasoline-truck mileage for two to three trucks:

`3,900–15,600 × 2–3 trucks = 7,800–46,800 miles`

Duplicate truck mileage removed by consolidating two to three trucks into one:

`3,900–15,600 × 1–2 avoided trucks = 3,900–31,200 miles`

## Gasoline emissions

EPA’s comparison method uses 8,887 grams of tailpipe CO₂ per gallon and multiplies tailpipe emissions by 1.25 to include average upstream gasoline production and distribution.

At a 15–20 mpg pickup baseline:

`7,800 miles ÷ 20 mpg × 8.887 kg/gallon × 1.25 = 4.3 tCO₂e`

`46,800 miles ÷ 15 mpg × 8.887 kg/gallon × 1.25 = 34.7 tCO₂e`

Rounded page range: **approximately 4–35 metric tons CO₂e avoided**.

The TELO side is treated as zero tailpipe and near-zero marginal operational charging emissions only if its charging is matched to otherwise-surplus onsite solar. Vehicle manufacturing, battery manufacturing, solar equipment, charging losses, and backup-grid energy are not zero and must be included if the comparison uses a lifecycle boundary.

## Cost proxy

The duplicate-travel calculation uses the IRS business mileage rate effective July 1, 2026—$0.76 per mile—as a broad proxy for vehicle ownership and operating cost:

`3,900–31,200 duplicate miles × $0.76/mile = $2,964–$23,712`

Rounded page range: **approximately $3,000–$24,000 before crew travel time**.

This is not projected cash savings. Replace the proxy with actual depreciation, financing, insurance, maintenance, tires, gasoline, electricity, and labor-time costs before using the figure commercially.

## Building comparison

The Carbon Leadership Forum’s policy calculator publishes single-family residential baselines by building scope. Those benchmarks establish that compact-home upfront embodied carbon is of the same order of magnitude as the upper travel scenario, but they are not a result for a Just Haus project.

Before stating that avoided travel exceeds the building itself, calculate the project’s actual A1–A5 embodied carbon from material quantities and environmental product declarations using a recognized whole-building method or EC3. Keep floor area, included systems, biogenic carbon, site work, construction waste, and lifecycle stages consistent and explicit.
