# First-call talking points

## Desired outcome

Learn what TELO needs from early commercial customers and determine whether a scoped field relationship is useful. Do not negotiate delivery priority on the first call.

## 60-second introduction

- Just Haus is an early-stage design-build venture in Franconia, New Hampshire.
- The work centers on compact, efficient homes and solar, batteries, inverters, resilience, and off-grid systems.
- Daniel is an engineer, builder, experienced EV-truck owner, off-grid energy-system user, and early MT1 reservation holder.
- The MT1 would perform real construction work in a sustained northern environment.
- Years of winter Tesla use provide concrete comparative experience with cold-weather usability and reliability frustrations.
- Daniel is willing to contribute field-testing time and structured feedback without consulting fees; this is not an offer to absorb vehicle costs, direct expenses, insurance, or liability.

## The use case

- Material and tool transport, sheet goods, supplier runs, site visits, and towing within verified ratings.
- Deep winter, prolonged cold soak, snow, repeated icing, mountain grades, rural roads, and cross-border travel through Quebec and New Brunswick.
- Specific usability checks for windshield de-icing, wipers, washer sprayers, frozen handles, seals, latches, sensors, and tailgate or liftgate operation.
- Charging around solar production, stationary storage, inverter limits, and rural infrastructure.
- Repeatable routes and consistent logs that distinguish temperature, load, grade, and charging conditions.

## Questions for TELO

1. Which commercial use cases or operating environments are most valuable during launch?
2. Does TELO have a formal early-customer, advisory, beta, field-validation, or case-study program?
3. What feedback format and cadence would be useful to product and engineering teams?
4. What vehicle data can customers access, and what privacy or confidentiality boundaries apply?
5. Which payload, towing, charging, and power-export capabilities are confirmed for the relevant configuration?
6. Are there service, winter-tire, roadside-support, or cross-border constraints to plan around?
7. What would make a field relationship successful after 3, 6, and 12 months?
8. Which winter failure modes, usability concerns, or validation gaps does TELO most want an experienced EV owner to investigate?

## Possible engagement shapes

- Early commercial customer with structured feedback.
- Cold-climate field-use participant.
- Customer advisory member.
- Construction use-case collaborator.
- Documented case study after sufficient real-world use.

## Boundaries to state clearly

- Just Haus is early-stage; do not overstate project volume or audience reach.
- Public content will identify the concept as independent unless TELO approves other wording.
- No unsupported claim about power export, V2L, V2H, towing, payload, range, delivery timing, or launch allocation.
- Agree on data ownership, publication approval, confidentiality, expenses, support, and vehicle/service responsibilities before any formal program.
- Keep Tesla comparisons factual and experience-based; describe specific observed conditions rather than making broad claims about the brand.

## Close

Ask for the smallest concrete next step: a technical follow-up, commercial-program introduction, requested information packet, or date to reconnect.
