# Voice and brand

Public language is direct, informal, curious, and playful, and it stays on what is possible.

## Voice

- Say what Just Haus is, and what a project can become.
- Name a goal as a goal, and a test as a test.
- A number waits until it exists and is ready to share.
- Claims stay specific.

## Words

| On the page | Off the page |
| --- | --- |
| Comfort, Health, Efficiency, Sustainability, when they name a real aim | affordable; bad; the line "Always becoming more" |
