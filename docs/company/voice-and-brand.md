# Voice and brand

## Voice

Direct, informal, curious, and playful. The vibe is yes: everything is possible. Say what Just Haus is, and what a project can become.

Skip negative framing. Skip hype and unsupported claims. A goal can be named as a goal. A test can be named as a test. A number waits until it exists and is ready to share.

## Words to skip

- affordable
- bad
- the line "Always becoming more"

Value words that do belong: **Comfort, Health, Efficiency, Sustainability**. Use them when they name a real aim of the work, not as a slogan stack.

## Aesthetic

Japandi, with a quiet cyberpunk thread.

- Materials to reach for: birch, yakisugi, oiled pine, linen.
- Light: a subtle, indirect LED glow. Not a spectacle.
- Ground: dark, near-black. The live site is that dark room, phone first.
- The mark is the simple pitched-roof house with a door cutout. The wordmark in use is JUSTHA.US, alongside the name Just Haus.

The live pages are the reference for what is actually on screen. This note is the direction to keep in mind.

## Business card

Front: the house mark and the wordmark.

Back: "I love talking houses. Let's grab a drink and chat, it's on me."

The QR code opens <https://justha.us/?ref=card>. That link can show the one-time welcome on the site: a hello, a look around, and an invitation to share a house idea.
