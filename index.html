Just Haus is defined by what is removed. Fewer parts to buy, haul, fit, maintain, and replace. Extreme minimalism is the idea. It is a way of working, and the plan stays open.

The company is a New Hampshire design and build practice in the White Mountains, close to real buildings. Each one is a home and a test. The lesson can change the next one.
