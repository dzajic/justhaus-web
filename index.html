JUSTHA.US

A system for discovering better ways to build houses.

Nature sets the forces. Just Haus applies leverage.

Just Haus is defined by what is removed, not by what is added. Fewer parts to buy, haul, fit, maintain, and replace. Extreme minimalism is the idea. It is a way of working, not a look and not one fixed plan.

The company is a New Hampshire design and build practice, still small, tied to real buildings in the White Mountains. Each one is a home and a test. The lesson is supposed to change the next one.

Always becoming more.

Approach Subtraction, experiments, and buildings that can be improved. Projects Alpha is underway. Later work is still a plan. Experiments What is being tested, including the parts that fail.
