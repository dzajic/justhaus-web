About

Daniel Zajic

Just Haus is Daniel Zajic, building Just Haus Alpha in Franconia, New Hampshire.

The project comes from a habit of taking buildings apart and wanting the next one to be simpler. Years of renovating older houses led to this one: designed after four architects had drawn their versions, and built by him. The house is still an experiment. The notes on how it is going are in the journal.

To follow the work, write. There is no newsletter on this site.
