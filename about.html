About

Daniel Zajic

Just Haus LLC is his design and build company. He founded it. He is the one doing the building.

The work sits in the White Mountains of New Hampshire, on buildings he can watch through a winter. He also works in software. This site is about the buildings.

Just Haus is not a catalog, a prefab line, or a finished method. It is a practice for discovering better ways to build, one project at a time. Alpha is the first of those projects.
