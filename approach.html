Approach

Extreme minimalism is the idea.

Take out what the building can do without, and let the next one keep what holds.

  • Take things out

    Fewer things to buy, handle, install, maintain, and replace. Assemblies that open, and panels come off, so repair and rearrangement belong in the design.

  • Buildings are experiments

    A project is a home and a test at the same time. It is like a dozen, on a busy day.

  • The next one inherits the lesson

    Just Haus Alpha is the first building. What it teaches is meant to change whatever comes after.

  • Warm, on purpose

    Minimal, and still warm. Quiet architecture, wood that weathers, daylight, and the life in the rooms. Lived-in is the goal.

  • A real winter

    The place is the White Mountains. Snow, moisture, and a long heating season ask the questions. Energy is a set of tradeoffs, chosen for a real winter.

  • Technology that simplifies the day

    A system earns its place when daily life gets simpler.
