Field-use plan

Independent · unofficial concept

Compact electric utility for building in the North.

A concept for putting a TELO MT1 to work across residential construction, deep-winter roads, and solar-powered rural properties—then turning that use into structured, useful field evidence.

Why it fits

Right-sized by the same logic as a good building.

Just Haus is exploring compact, highly efficient homes shaped around real use rather than excess. The MT1’s proposition—a small exterior footprint with meaningful truck utility—speaks to the same discipline.

Here, compactness would not be a lifestyle accessory. It would be tested against lumber runs, tools, sheet goods, towing, rural travel, site visits, and daily work in conditions that quickly expose what is useful.

01 · Compact

Northern test environment

Conditions that cannot be reproduced in a short demo.

The White Mountains of New Hampshire—and regular travel through Quebec and New Brunswick—offer sustained exposure to deep cold, prolonged cold soak, snow, repeated icing, mountain grades, rough pavement, and limited rural charging.

Cold-soaked batteryRange, available power, regeneration, preconditioning, and charging after hours or days below freezing.
Ice managementWindshield clearing, wiper performance, washer-fluid delivery, frozen handles, and reliable access after storms.
Winter mechanismsTailgate or liftgate operation, seals, latches, cameras, sensors, and clearance with packed snow and ice.

Property energy

A truck inside a working energy system.

Just Haus properties are a practical environment for observing vehicle charging alongside solar generation, battery storage, inverters, household loads, and off-grid constraints—not as a showroom demonstration, but as day-to-day operation.

Solar-aware chargingSchedule and measure charging around surplus production, weather, storage state, and upcoming driving needs.
Load managementDocument the decisions required when vehicle charging shares finite inverter and service capacity with a home.
Power-export potentialIf TELO offers supported onboard power, V2L, or future bidirectional capability, test defined tool, temporary-load, or backup scenarios. No such capability is assumed here.

Construction field-use plan

Use it as a truck. Measure what matters.

Materials and payload

Repeatable routes and practical loading configurations.

  • Sheet goods, lumber, insulation, and finish materials
  • Tools, site equipment, and daily carry
  • Loaded versus unloaded energy use

Towing and access

Work travel across rural and mountain conditions.

  • Trailer use within verified vehicle ratings
  • Mountain grades and unpaved site access
  • Ground clearance and snow-traction observations

Site operations

Daily usefulness beyond moving people.

  • Site visits, estimating, and supplier runs
  • Cold starts and winter workdays
  • Temporary power only where officially supported

Long-term record

Consistent documentation instead of one-off impressions.

  • Seasonal efficiency and charging logs
  • Cold-soak, icing, wiper, washer, handle, latch, and tailgate notes
  • Photography and case-study-ready field reports
