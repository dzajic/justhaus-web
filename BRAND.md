# Just Haus — current brand reference

**Status:** Working guidance, 2026-10-11. Brand direction is not a finalized identity manual.
**History and evidence:** [docs/brand/just-haus-brief.md](docs/brand/just-haus-brief.md); [docs/brand/decisions.md](docs/brand/decisions.md).

## Core
Just Haus LLC is an early-stage New Hampshire design/build venture. The founder's accepted summary is: **“Just Haus is a system for discovering better ways to build houses.”** The work uses actual construction as a laboratory for better building systems. It is not yet a catalogue of commercially proven home models.

**Guiding idea:** “Just Haus is defined by what's removed not by what's added.” Simplify assemblies, reduce construction operations and unnecessary complexity, and make buildings adaptable, inspectable and repairable. Decisions are judged at the system level: performance, materials, cost, labor, maintenance, and real cold-climate use.

## Personality and voice
Direct, practical, curious, thoughtful, technically literate and occasionally playful. Warm architectural minimalism rather than luxury advertising or cold high-tech spectacle. Describe experiments, successes, failures and unresolved tradeoffs candidly. Use understandable language instead of jargon, hype, and generic green-building slogans. Never imply every experiment worked or that Just Haus has a large team, a licensed architecture practice, guaranteed savings, or a finished production design.

## Brand forms
- **Company:** Just Haus LLC; ordinary readable name **Just Haus**.
- **Domain:** https://justha.us
- **Explored/enthusiastically approved wordmark direction:** **JUSTHA.US**, letting the brand name double as the URL. Do not silently eliminate the regular “Just Haus” form.
- **Actual mark:** extremely simple pitched-roof house/cabin outline with a centered door cutout at the bottom; no windows. Use the real asset, not a replacement drawn from a prompt.
- **Approved visual exploration:** vertical-slat motif complementing the original house mark.
- **Color proposal, not final tokens:** charcoal `#111111`, warm ivory `#F3EFE6`, taupe `#B7A58E`. Existing repo also describes black, white, gray and warm natural accents. No locked final palette.
- **Type:** clean modern sans serif, generous space, controlled hierarchy; no final font selected.
- **Images:** real build photography, natural materials, exposed wood, dark wood/PV façade, construction details, weather and northern surroundings. Distinguish conceptual images from actual photos.
- **Project identities:** Just Haus Alpha is the first project's emerging identity; later Greek-letter project names/logos were positively received but are not an adopted numbering standard.

## Messaging guardrails
A useful, founder-approved sentence is “Nature sets the forces. Just Haus applies leverage.” Another direct founder statement: “Extreme minimalism is the idea.” Previous card copy (“Thoughtfully designed”, “Justly built”, “Made to last”, “Always becoming more”) was liked but should not be treated as a locked tagline set.

Current website should explain the mission, show real work, distinguish built from planned, and give visitors a credible introduction and contact route. Its single primary visitor conversion goal has **not** been selected.
