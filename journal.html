Experiments

Learn by building. Keep the misses.

The questions below are in play on Just Haus Alpha. None of them is a finished method, and this page does not keep score.

Being tested

What the foundation can do without

How much of the usual basement and the usual concrete the building can skip, in this climate, and still sit there honestly.

Being tested

Insulation, and keeping it on the building

A thick coat is the easy idea. The weather has edited it. Foam that winds up in the yard is still part of the lesson.

Being tested

Interiors that can come apart

Wood, and panels that are meant to be removed, so a change does not start with demolition. Drywall is the thing being avoided where it can be.

Being tested

Air, on purpose

A tight building only works if fresh air is a decision. How that system should be is still being learned in use.

Being tested

Energy for a snowy season

Making power on the building, storing some of it, and spending it in a cold climate. The interesting question is the tradeoff, not a record output. Figures are not published here.

Being tested

The order of the work

Sequence is its own experiment: what one person can do, in what order, without burying a problem inside a finished wall.
