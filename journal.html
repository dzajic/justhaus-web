Experiments

Learn by building. Write it all down.

The questions below are in play on Just Haus Alpha. Each one is still being learned, in the open.

Being tested

A simpler foundation

Less basement and less concrete, for this climate, watched to see how honestly it sits.

Being tested

Insulation, and keeping it on the building

A thick coat is the easy idea. The weather has edited it. Foam that winds up in the yard is still part of the lesson.

Being tested

Interiors that can come apart

Wood, and panels that are meant to come off, so a change starts by opening the wall. Where it can, the interior stays in wood.

Being tested

Air, on purpose

A tight building only works if fresh air is a decision. How that system should be is still being learned in use.

Being tested

Energy for a snowy season

Making power on the building, storing some of it, and spending it in a cold climate. The interesting question is the tradeoff. Alpha will be measured, and the results shared here as they come in.

Being tested

The order of the work

Sequence is its own experiment: what one person can do, in what order, with every question still visible when the wall closes.
