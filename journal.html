Experiments

Learn by building. Write it all down.

These questions are in play on Just Haus Alpha. Each one is still being learned.

A simpler foundation

How little basement and concrete this climate really needs, and how well the building rests on it.

Insulation, and keeping it on the building

A thick coat is the easy idea. The weather has edited it, and foam that winds up in the yard is still part of the lesson.

Interiors that can come apart

Wood, and panels meant to come off, so a change starts by opening the wall.

Air, on purpose

A tight building asks for fresh air on purpose, and how that system should be is still being learned in use.

Energy for a snowy season

Making power on the building, storing some of it, and spending it in a cold climate is the tradeoff. The results will be shared here as they come in.

The order of the work

Sequence is its own experiment: what one person can do, in what order, with every question still visible when the wall closes.
