Build journal

What has happened so far

Notes from the construction of Just Haus Alpha. The photographs are from the build, including the set used in the Science on Screen talk. The reasons for the choices are on the approach page.

The foundation, without a basement

A basement fought the rest of the project: cost, labor, concrete, and moisture. The question was whether one was needed. The work in these photographs is a frost-protected shallow foundation. Insulation protects the soil. There is less excavation, less concrete, and a wall one person can build.

The blocks are Fox Blocks insulated concrete forms, set in a prepared trench. Early in the work, a canopy on the site was destroyed by wind in a couple of days.

Foam outside the frame

Almost all of the insulation is outside the structure: about 6 inches of EPS and 1 inch of polyiso, on the order of R-33. The simple idea was to put the coat on the outside of the frame.

Keeping it on the building was the harder problem. Sheets of foam blew around the site, and roof panels came off in the wind, before the layer was fastened and covered. In the photograph, wood siding is going on over that foam, held off the foam by dark furring strips.

Wood inside, and a batt from Maine

The interior and the cladding are wood: local pine and other domestic lumber, detailed so a wall can be opened and repaired instead of demolished. The same record includes wood-fiber batts made in Maine. TimberHP is the product in the photograph.

That batt is not the thick exterior layer. The outside coat on this house is the foam. A carbon account built on wood fiber would describe a different assembly, and it is not claimed for this one.

Still underway

The October photograph shows the walls in wood and the roof sheathing just started, with studs still open to the weather. Another view shows sheathing and housewrap further along. Two years into the work, the house is becoming the building he had drawn, and it is not finished.

A separate garage is underway. More photographs are in the build album.
