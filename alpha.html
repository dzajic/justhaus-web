Just Haus Alpha

The first project. Thinking outside the box, or in this case the cube. The name on the building is Alpha.

Alpha is a house Daniel Zajic is building, and revising as he learns. The lessons are still open. It is not a model for sale, and it is not offered as a finished design.

For a while he called it the cube. That was a nickname. Alpha is the name, and later projects may take the next letters if that convention holds. It is not settled.

What is being tried, and what has already gone sideways, is on the experiments page. The rest of the lineup is on projects.
